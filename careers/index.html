Currently recruiting: Proficient and Advanced Practitioners, and a PBS Lead

AkindaCo is expanding its Positive Behaviour Support team across Adelaide. Current opportunities

Three positions across the practitioner pathway.

Every position is defined against the NDIS Positive Behaviour Support Capability Framework. AkindaCo is committed to career progression within the organisation, and wherever possible senior positions are filled through internal promotion.

  • Proficient Practitioner

    A clinical position for a Proficient practitioner seeking progression to Advanced level. The practitioner holds a caseload across home, school and community settings, with supervision, professional development and case mix selected to support progression along a mapped pathway.

    Part-time or full-time. Hybrid. From $75,000 pro rata.

  • Advanced Practitioner

    A senior clinical position for an Advanced practitioner who wishes to combine complex casework with the development of others. The practitioner carries the more complex presentations, provides clinical supervision and mentoring to less experienced practitioners, and contributes to the internal training program, without operational management responsibilities.

    Part-time or full-time. Hybrid. From $95,000 pro rata.

  • PBS Lead

    A clinical leadership position for a practitioner at Advanced or Specialist level who wishes to retain a caseload. The Lead holds clinical oversight of the behaviour support service, supervises the practitioner team, develops the training program and the progression pathway, and maintains the quality of behaviour support plans as the team grows.

    Part-time or full-time. Hybrid. Remuneration determined by capability level and scope. Caseload set in proportion to the leadership responsibilities.

Flexible and hybrid working

Arrangements designed for sustainable practice

Behaviour support attracts experienced clinicians, and experienced clinicians have commitments beyond work: family, study, further training, and in some cases a private practice of their own. Every position is therefore available part-time or full-time. Days and hours are agreed with the practitioner at appointment, and the caseload is then built to fit them.

Every position is also hybrid. Behaviour support is delivered where behaviour occurs, so part of each week is spent in homes, schools and supported accommodation. The remainder is divided between our clinic rooms and home-based days for assessment analysis, plan development, team liaison and telehealth.

The same structure allows us to welcome practitioners based in regional South Australia. Our service extends to regional participants, and a practitioner living outside metropolitan Adelaide can combine home-based work, telehealth and regional outreach with periodic clinic days, on an arrangement agreed individually at appointment.

Why join AkindaCo

Clinical practice, supported by structure.

The following apply to every position, at every level.

  • A systemic practice

    Family therapy, psychotherapy and counselling, art psychotherapy, early childhood services and support work are delivered within the same organisation as behaviour support. The people who implement a plan are frequently colleagues: in the same supervision group, at the same case conference, or seeing the same family. Practitioners can co-work with the therapist already engaged with a family, train the support worker who will deliver the strategies, and see the plan implemented as it was written.

  • Structured career progression

    AkindaCo is committed to career progression within the organisation, and wherever possible senior positions are filled through internal promotion. The pathway from Proficient to Advanced, and from Advanced into clinical leadership, is mapped against the Capability Framework, and supervision, training and case mix are selected to support each practitioner's progression. Remuneration is reviewed as capability level changes.

  • Clinical supervision and professional development

    Structured group supervision and individual clinical supervision commence in the first week of employment and are delivered by senior behaviour support practitioners. Supervision is clinical in focus: formulation, complex presentations, restrictive practice decisions and the clinical judgement on which a plan depends. Practitioners also draw on the expertise of the practice's family therapists, whose systemic perspective informs the behaviour support work.

  • Protected clinical time

    Practitioner positions carry no operational or people-management responsibilities, and the Lead position is clinical leadership rather than practice administration. An in-house administration team manages scheduling, invoicing and service agreements, and documentation workflows are AI-assisted, so that the routine elements of a report are prepared from the practitioner's assessment rather than written from the beginning.

  • A graduated billable expectation

    For practitioner positions the billable expectation is 24 hours per week at full-time, pro rata for part-time. The expectation is graduated over the first months of employment so that a caseload can be established properly.

  • An evidence-based framework

    Clinical practice at AkindaCo is grounded in the Multi-Element Behaviour Support framework. Plans address the environment, skill development, relationships and reactive strategies together, rather than relying on a single intervention. Practitioners who wish to work in this way, with the structure and supervision to support it, will find that structure already in place.

The clinical work

Assessment, formulation, and plans that reach implementation.

Practitioners hold a caseload of NDIS participants across home, school and community settings. The caseload spans children through to adults and includes complex presentations in which the functional behaviour assessment is decisive.

  • Functional behaviour assessment and formulation
  • Interim and comprehensive behaviour support plans
  • Strategies trialled with the participant before they are written into the plan
  • Training families, support workers, teachers and school staff in the plan
  • Identification, reporting and reduction of restrictive practices
  • Measurement of frequency and episodic severity so that progress can be evidenced
  • Case conferences, plan reviews and school meetings, attended in person
  • At Advanced and Lead level: clinical supervision, mentoring and the internal training program

What we look for

The practitioners we are seeking.

  • A Behaviour Support Practitioner at Proficient level or above whose next career stage is deeper clinical practice or clinical leadership, rather than a move away from practice
  • An allied health qualification, with experience in a therapy role prior to behaviour support
  • A strong grounding in functional behaviour assessment and evidence-based, person-centred practice, and a genuine interest in working within the Multi-Element Behaviour Support framework
  • A collaborative approach to working with therapists and support workers as colleagues in the implementation of a plan
  • The capacity to hold a caseload with autonomy across home, school and community settings
  • A commitment to collaborative supervision and to a team culture over hierarchy
  • Fluency in Auslan is highly regarded, though not required
  • A current NDIS Worker Screening clearance and a Working With Children Check. Both are essential.

About AkindaCo

One team around the participant.

AkindaCo is a registered NDIS provider and specialist behaviour support provider based in North Adelaide and Salisbury. We are a systemic practice: behaviour support, family therapy, psychotherapy and counselling, art psychotherapy, early childhood services and support work are delivered by one organisation, so that the people around a participant work from a shared understanding.

  • A practice shaped by its clinicians

    AkindaCo is small enough that a practitioner's clinical perspective influences how the practice operates, with direct access to the directors.

  • Established structure

    Supervision, administration, clinical systems, an in-house support work team trained by our own practitioners, and a referral pipeline that sustains a full caseload are all in place.

  • Grounded in evidence

    Members of Behaviour Support Practitioners Australia. Practice built on the published Multi-Element Behaviour Support evidence base.

    Email your application

We work with Deaf participants and particularly encourage applications from Auslan users. Adjustments to the application process are available on request.

Frequently asked questions

Information for prospective applicants.

Are all of the positions available part-time?

Yes, including the Lead position. Days and hours are agreed at appointment and the caseload and billable expectation are set pro rata. Part-time practitioners receive the same supervision, progression pathway and administrative support as full-time practitioners.

What does hybrid working involve?

Each week combines clinic days at North Adelaide or Salisbury, outreach to homes, schools and supported accommodation, and home-based days for plan development, analysis, team liaison and telehealth. The balance depends on the caseload and the practitioner's days, and is agreed rather than imposed.

I am based in regional South Australia. Can I apply?

Yes. AkindaCo provides behaviour support to participants in regional South Australia as well as metropolitan Adelaide, and the hybrid structure of the positions makes a regional base workable. A regionally based practitioner would combine home-based work, telehealth and outreach to regional participants with periodic clinic days in Adelaide. The specific arrangement, including travel, is agreed individually at appointment.

Does the PBS Lead position involve leaving clinical practice?

No. The Lead retains a caseload, sized to allow for clinical oversight, supervision and the training program. The position is clinical leadership rather than practice administration. Rostering, invoicing and operations sit with the administration and management team.

Is the caseload limited to children?

No. The majority of our work is with children and young people, and we also work with adult participants, including adults in supported accommodation and adults with psychosocial disability. Case mix is one of the elements selected with a practitioner's progression in mind.
